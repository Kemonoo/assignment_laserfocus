How the mathematics works

Each reel independently selects one of 20 equally likely stops. Only the three center symbols are evaluated.

A win requires an exact triple. Rarer triples pay more. RTP is the expected long-run return across the complete model—not a promise for a short session.

Σ (winning ways × payout) ÷ 20³ = 7,296 ÷ 8,000 = 91.2%
