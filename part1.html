Process & methodology · Part 01

Navigation & Layout Modernization

1. Problem statement

  • Outdated visuals: The original app interface feels dated and visually cluttered.
  • Messy navigation: Referrals occupied prime space in the bottom navigation despite being a secondary feature, taking attention away from the core gameplay flow.
  • Missing settings: There was no dedicated Settings menu where players could adjust audio, manage their account, or access other preferences.

2. Proposed solution

  • Navigation-bar cleanup: I removed Referrals from the bottom bar and centered the Play button to give the primary action more emphasis. The core tabs are now Shop, Competitions, Profile, Team, and Play.
  • New Settings menu: I added a clear Settings button in the top-right corner. It now contains Referrals, audio controls, and account-management tools.
  • Visual refresh: I designed two modern theme variations to demonstrate a cleaner, more contemporary visual direction.

3. Measuring success

Design changes are partly subjective, so their impact is difficult to judge confidently before release. The strongest evaluation combines direct player feedback with behavioral data after the update goes live.

  • Player feedback: Ask the community directly and review responses to the new visual design and navigation.
  • Engagement statistics: Compare metrics such as Daily Active Users, session length, and revenue before and after the update.
  • Feature usage: Track whether players use the new Settings menu for actions such as muting the game or linking accounts, and measure how relocating Referrals affects its usage.

4. Process & methodology

  • Recreation: I took raw screenshots of the original app and used Claude Design to rebuild them as clean, editable interface frames.
  • Layout adjustments: I experimented with the bottom bar to find the clearest way to center the main gameplay loop and remove unnecessary visual weight.
  • Implementation: I selected the two strongest modern themes and converted them into working HTML/CSS prototypes so the redesigns can be viewed and interacted with directly.
