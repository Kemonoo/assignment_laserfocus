Process & methodology · Part 01

Navigation & Layout Modernization

Overview & objectives

The redesign replaces rigid, high-friction interface elements with a cleaner and more cohesive bottom navigation system. The goal was to reduce visual noise while keeping the most important destinations within comfortable thumb reach.

The restructured navigation covers Shop, Competitions, Profile, Team, Play, and Settings.

Design & AI execution

  1. Recreation and ideation: I supplied raw application screenshots and used Claude Design to recreate their layout as clean, editable interface frames.
  2. Iterative exploration: I compared several bottom-bar configurations to establish a clear hierarchy and improve one-handed navigation.
  3. Selection and implementation: I selected the two strongest variations, prioritizing minimal visual noise and easy access to the primary action, and converted them into working HTML/CSS presentations.
